5 · Does machine learning improve the forecast?

Selection used FY2023–24 validation MAE. The chosen day-adjusted naive model also outperformed random forest in FY2024–25. Each prediction uses only prior monthly observations.

A trial band based on the 80th percentile of validation errors covered only 5 of 12 test outcomes. It is inadequate for operational service commitments. The April 2025 demonstration forecast is 12,094 movements, using data through March 2025; it is not a current forecast and publication vintages were not reconstructed.

Text analysis and retrieval method

Python TF-IDF ranks distinctive words and bigrams in the curated summaries. Human-assigned themes cover throughput, reliability, data quality, capacity, comparability, connectivity, evaluation and forecasting. These are analyst-selected evidence themes, not statistically representative literature frequencies. The browser chatbot uses token TF-IDF cosine similarity with a minimum relevance threshold.

    Important limits of interpretation

    No random assignment, comparable untreated route panel, train-level dwell data or matched route lengths were available. National Indian Railways averages and DFC averages describe different traffic populations. Staged commissioning, trip mix, COVID effects and other railway investments confound causal comparisons. No synthetic records were added to observed data. Scenario and cost inputs are clearly separated. The forecast model is an academic retrospective demonstration, not a railway control system.
